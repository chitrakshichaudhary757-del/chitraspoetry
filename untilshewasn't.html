She Was Soft Until She Wasn’t

She was soft—
like morning light through half-open curtains,
like hands that forgave
before they were even asked to.

She believed in forever
the way children believe in magic.
Wholeheartedly.
Without backup plans.

Her voice trembled
not from weakness,
but from caring too much.

She stayed.
She understood.
She made excuses for storms
that were never hers to calm.

And then one day—
she grew tired
of being the only gentle thing
in a room full of sharp edges.

No one noticed the shift at first.

Softness doesn’t disappear loudly.
It fades.
Thread by thread.

She stopped explaining.
Stopped overthinking.
Stopped apologizing for feelings
that were never wrong.

Her heart, once an open window,
became a door
that required knocking.

She did not become cruel.
She became careful.

She did not stop loving.
She started loving herself too.

And the world said,
"She has changed."

Yes.
She did.

She was soft—
until she realized
being soft for everyone
was slowly breaking her.

So she chose strength.

Not the loud kind.
Not the angry kind.

But the quiet kind
that walks away
without trembling.
