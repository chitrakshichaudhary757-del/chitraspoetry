UNDER CONSTRUCTION

I am losing myself
on the way to becoming a strong woman.

Not all at once—
but in quiet fragments.

In the way I no longer beg to be understood.
In the way I swallow tears
and call it discipline.
In the way my heart now pauses
before it trusts.

They say strength is beautiful.
But they never speak of the funerals—
the soft-spoken girl
laid to rest beneath expectations.

I did not wake up powerful.
I was carved into it.
By disappointment.
By silence.
By learning the language of leaving.

If I seem distant now,
it is because I am rebuilding
from the inside out.

I miss her sometimes—
the softness,
the innocence,
the easy laughter.

But I cannot return.

Yes, I am losing parts of myself—
but I am not lost.

I am under construction.
